About Curlmoon

Most dogs sleep curled up, nose tucked in, with something to lean against. Curlmoon exists for them, and for the people who share their home with them.

We make one thing and put all of our attention on it: the Curlmoon Nest, a round bed with a raised rim all the way around, a deep, soft center and long faux fur. It comes in four sizes for dogs up to about 30 lb and in three calm colors that look good where they stand, so the bed never has to hide in a corner.

Why we exist

A flat bed gives a dog nothing to lean on, so many dogs end up on the couch, the armrest or the laundry pile. We wanted a bed that matches the way dogs actually rest: curled up, chin on something soft. Three things guide everything we do: we tell you the truth about what you are buying, we deliver what we promise, and we make it right when something goes wrong.

Our service promise

A real person reads every message and we reply within one business day. If your dog does not take to the Nest within 30 days of delivery, or the size does not fit, you get a full refund and you keep the bed. Nothing to send back.

Shipping you can plan around

Shipping is free on every US order, and customs duties are already included, so there is nothing to pay at your door. Every order ships with tracking. The bed arrives vacuum-packed: rub the filling evenly, give it 6 to 12 hours, and it fluffs back to full size.

Welcome

If you have a question about sizing, shipping or anything else, write to us at support@curlmoon.com. We are glad to help you pick the right size.
