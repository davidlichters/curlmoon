Shipping policy

At a glance: free US shipping · ships within 1 to 2 business days · arrives 6 to 10 business days later · customs duties and taxes included · tracking on every order.

Shipping rates

Shipping is free on every order to the United States. All customs duties and import taxes are already included in the price. You will never be asked to pay anything at delivery.

Order confirmation

As soon as you place your order, you receive a confirmation email with your order number. If it does not arrive within a few minutes, please check your spam folder or contact us.

Processing time

We hand your order to the carrier within 1 to 2 business days. Business days are Monday to Friday, excluding U.S. holidays.

Delivery time

After your order is handed to the carrier, delivery takes 6 to 10 business days within the contiguous United States. You get a tracking link by email as soon as it ships.

How it arrives

The bed ships vacuum-packed so it travels compact and protected. Unpack it, rub the filling evenly and give it 6 to 12 hours to fluff up; the biggest size can take up to 48 hours.

Gifts

Sending it as a gift? Enter the recipient's address as the shipping address at checkout.

Questions

Email support@curlmoon.com. We reply within 1 business day.
