Which size should I get?

Pick by weight: 16" for dogs up to 5 lb, 20" up to 11 lb, 24" up to 22 lb, 28" up to 30 lb. If your dog likes to stretch out, go one size up. Not sure? Email support@porchnap.com with their weight and we will tell you honestly which size fits.

Will my dog actually use it?

If your dog sleeps curled up or rests their chin on the sofa armrest, there is a good chance. And if they do not take to it within 30 days, you get a full refund and keep the bed.

Why does it look flat when it arrives?

The bed ships vacuum-packed. Shake it out and give it 6 to 12 hours to fluff back up.

Can I wash it?

Yes. Wash it gently, let it air dry, then shake it to fluff the fur back up.

What if my dog does not use it?

Email support@porchnap.com within 30 days of delivery with your order number and a photo of the bed. You get a full refund of the price of the bed and you keep it. One refund per household.

How long does shipping take?

Shipping is free on every US order and comes with tracking. Delivery takes 6 to 10 business days.

Will it fit in a crate?

Measure the floor of the crate. The bed is round, so it needs its full diameter both ways: 16", 20", 24" or 28".

Is it good for older dogs?

Many older dogs love a soft spot with something to rest the head on. It is a plush bed, not a firm orthopedic mattress, so if your vet asked for firm support, choose an orthopedic bed instead.

Is it only for dogs?

It is made for dogs up to about 30 lb, and plenty of cats claim it too.
