FAQ

Questions? Answered.

Which size should I get?

Pick by weight: 16" for dogs up to 5 lb, 20" up to 11 lb, 24" up to 22 lb, 28" up to 30 lb. Or measure your dog curled up, nose to base of tail, and add about 4 in. Between two sizes, size up. Wrong size is covered by the money-back guarantee.

Will my dog actually use it?

We cannot promise that, every dog is different. Tip: put an old blanket or a T-shirt that smells like you inside and give it 2 to 3 days. If your dog still does not take to it within 30 days, you get a full refund and keep the bed.

Why does it look flat when it arrives?

It ships vacuum-packed. Unpack it, rub the filling evenly with your hands and let it rest, in the sun if you can. It fluffs up within 6 to 12 hours, the biggest size can take up to 48 hours.

Can I wash it?

Yes. Wash it gently and let it air dry. Please do not put it in the dryer. Shake it afterwards to fluff the fur back up.

Is it chew-proof?

No. It is a soft plush bed. If your dog shreds beds, this is not the right one.

When will it arrive?

We hand your order to the carrier within 1 to 2 business days, delivery takes 6 to 10 business days after that. Shipping is free, customs duties are included, and you get a tracking link by email.

Can I send it as a gift?

Yes. Enter the recipient's address as the shipping address at checkout.

Is it good for older dogs?

Many older dogs love a soft spot with something to rest the head on. It is a plush bed, not a firm orthopedic mattress, so if your vet asked for firm support, choose an orthopedic bed instead.
